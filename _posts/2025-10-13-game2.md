## Wu-Tang: Rise of the Deceiver

<img src="/assets/WuTangRotD.jpg" class="display-img" alt="Wu-Tang: Rise of the Deceiver Key Art"/>
UE5 | PC ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3763470/WuTang_Rise_of_the_Deceiver/){:target="_blank"}, [Epic](https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/p/wutang-rise-of-the-deceiver-53c680){:target="_blank"}) | TBA | [Project Site](https://brasslionentertainment.com/){:target="_blank"}
{: style="color:DarkGray; font-size: 80%; text-align: center;"}

Wu-Tang: Rise of the Deceiver is a co-op action RPG where players harness the mystical abilities of the Wu-Tang Clan to fight against the Deceiver, an entity invading and corrupting their home of Shaolin.

Role: Gameplay Programmer
- Implemented multiplayer combat systems using GAS (Gameplay Ability System) with a data-driven architecture for combat designers and artists:
    - Anim Notifies for Animation Montages
    - Blueprint Libraries
    - Hit Resolution System
    - Status Effects System
    - Multiplayer Scaling System
- Maintained and refined existing combat systems:
    - Input System
    - Hitbox and Hit Detection
    - Hitstop
    - Enemy AI Behavior Trees and AI Controllers
- Worked closely with designers to develop bespoke gameplay mechanics thematically based off of the Wu-Tang Clan members.

## Silent Slayer

## Lost Recipes

<img src="/assets/LostRecipes.jpg" class="display-img" alt="Lost Recipes Key Art"/>
UE4 | Meta Quest ([Meta Horizon Store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/lost-recipes/4584847304916084/){:target="_blank"}) | Jan 2022 | [Project Site](https://schellgames.com/portfolio/lost-recipes){:target="_blank"}
{: style="color:DarkGray; font-size: 80%; text-align: center;"}

Lost Recipes is a VR cooking simulator that teaches historical recipes from ancient Greek, Chinese, and Mayan civilizations.

Role: Gameplay Programmer
- Took ownership of the following cooking systems mid-way through the project:
    - Dynamic mesh cutting and water mesh physics.
    - Osmosis, marination, spice rendering.
    - Dough systems.
- Implemented the following systems:
    - Multi-purpose spoon capable of stirring, scooping, pouring, and splitting depending on player intention and the interacted objects.
    - Plate and food stacking systems.
    - Pooling, state resetting, and respawning systems.
- Supported designers growing technical skills with Unreal blueprints.
- Optimized for the lower end Quest 1 VR system while primarily targeting the Quest 2 VR system.

## Deep Time Detectives

<img src="/assets/DeepTimeDetectives.jpg" class="display-img" alt="Deep Time Detectives Key Art"/>
Unity | PCVR + PC Exhibit | September 2019 | [Project Site](https://schellgames.com/portfolio/deep-time-detectives){:target="_blank"}
{: style="color:DarkGray; font-size: 80%; text-align: center;"}

Deep Time Detectives is a two-player asymmetric cooperative experience developed as part of the Smithsonian's hands-on pale­on­tology exhibit for their documentary film [When Whales Walked: Journeys in Deep Time](https://www.pbs.org/show/when-whales-walked-journeys-deep-time/){:target="_blank"}. The VR player acts as a field paleontologist, and the non-VR player acts as an offsite research expert to uncover a fossil and classify its evolutionary lineage.

Role: Gameplay Programmer
- Implemented fossil manipulation and brush interactions.
- Assisted with camera work for the VR rig and for the non-VR player.
- Assisted as tech support in installations across the country.
